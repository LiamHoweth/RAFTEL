## External signal: a successful placement can hide unwanted contact

**Author-reported preprint, submitted November 20:** [RoboPRO v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26971v1) evaluates bimanual manipulation in simulation, varying clutter as well as visual, instruction and object conditions. Its protocol separates task completion from contact with furniture and displacement of other objects. The authors report that clutter can reduce success and increase non-target collision. These are findings for their simulated environments and policies, not measurements on ARM-01 or proof that the paper's contact thresholds transfer to it.

## Practical takeaway: a paired path test

After the nominal target set and moving-geometry prerequisites, plan two offline fixtures with the same start and goal: one unobstructed and one with a declared obstacle that intersects the direct sweep between them while leaving both endpoints clear. Score endpoint convergence separately from whether a checked path was found. An alternate path may pass in the obstructed fixture; the original intersecting path must not be reported as clear. If geometry, registration or coverage is missing, report it as unknown, not a pass. This is a proposed regression, not a motion plan or new evidence. See the [roadmap's motion-planning gate](../docs/roadmap.md).
