## External signal: a bundled learning kit highlights the integration work

**Company announcement, November 24:** ASRock Industrial introduced RBK-100, combining local compute, an RGB-D camera, a six-axis leader/follower arm set and a workflow for recording demonstrations, reviewing data, training and executing learned skills. The announcement describes an offering, not independently measured reliability or evidence the stack or its models transfer to Thor.

**Inference for RAFTEL:** capture and review are separate deliverables from training. Borrow that ordering, not the hardware. An observation replay checks whether recorded inputs meet a contract; it cannot tell whether a camera was calibrated well or a grasp actually succeeded.

## Practical takeaway: one offline acceptance fixture

Before investing in learning from demonstrations, plan one synthetic pick-and-place record that preserves the original observation, proposed action and separately observed outcome. Add a paired case whose latest observation has a timestamp outside the declared freshness window. Keep its rejection and original observation in the report rather than counting an older accepted position as a successful recovery. This is a proposed offline fixture and does not change the kinematics priority or authorize physical data collection.
