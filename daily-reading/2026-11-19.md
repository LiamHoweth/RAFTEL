## Company watch: integration matters as much as fingers

SCHUNK's humanoid-hand spin-off announced on **November 18** that it now operates as TACTILIA Robotics. Its stated focus is a modular industrial hand usable across robot types, with development and customer testing supported by SCHUNK's CoLabs. The company emphasizes integration and continuous-use reliability. [Primary release](https://www.tactilia-robotics.com/tactilia-robotics-schunk-spin-off-robot-hand/).

This is a company announcement, not an independent reliability test. It provides no comparable BenchSort trial result, complete integration specification or purchase-price basis for changing our design.

**My takeaway:** keep the parallel gripper. Before discussing extra fingers, describe the jobs our two fingers must accomplish. The upgrade trigger should be a measured task limitation, not a competitor's hand shape.

## A small review exercise

For each of the proposed ten sorting objects, eventually record:

- the intended contact surfaces and approach direction;
- required opening and clearance, with measurement uncertainty;
- whether slip, rotation, crushing or failed release is the likely failure;
- what independent observation would establish a successful release into the correct box.

This is a proposed task-definition exercise, not evidence that these measurements exist. Start with the existing jaw and fabrication gates; do not infer safe payload or authorize a powered grasp from this checklist.
