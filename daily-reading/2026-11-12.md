## Read first: three decisions worth understanding

### 1. Check one model before trusting a full plate

The inspection plan starts with CommonBearingFix, recorded as approximately **30 × 15.780 × 4 mm**. Check scale, object count, bed contact and repair warnings before inspecting the eight-copy plate. Preserve any existing unsaved slicer scene.

The saved software profile is Kobra 3 Max / 0.4 mm / PLA / 0.20 mm Standard. Those selectors do not confirm the installed nozzle or actual spool. The prepared plate's 20 mm mesh-box gaps are useful inspection spacing; supports, brim, toolhead clearance, orientation and structural suitability still need review. [Inspection procedure and exact dimensions](../arm-01/docs/thor-anycubic-desktop-planning.md).

**For Liam:** actual nozzle, filament brand and bench/clamp dimensions remain more useful inputs than another assumed print setting. No print start or upload is part of this reading task.

### 2. Separate measured mass from a convincing-looking CAD number

The new printed-inertia study authenticates the placement of all 65 printed copies. Its **4.322 kg** result assumes completely uniform solid material at **1240 kg/m³** and covers only the printed subset. It is neither a sliced estimate nor a measured whole-arm mass. Do not compare it directly with yesterday's different whole-arm/fill-fraction scenario. [Study and scope](../arm-01/docs/thor-printed-inertia-study.md).

The new [measurement-readiness companion](../arm-01/docs/thor-measurement-readiness.md) helps record uncertainty, calibration declarations and supporting evidence. Its blank 65-row template has **zero observations ready for review**. Having a form and matching file hashes does not supply measurements.

**Takeaway:** retain separate columns for CAD assumptions, slicer estimates and eventual physical observations.

### 3. Treat an unknown outcome as unknown

The [fault-pack tests](../arm-01/tests/test_thor_fault_injection_study.py) construct two release failures with identical observed traces: in one synthetic world the object remains held; in the other it was released. Both keep held-object state unknown and claim zero successful sorts.

That is a useful design lesson for future BenchSort sensing: a command or missing acknowledgment cannot establish where the object ended up. The existing pack demonstrates this in software only; it adds no physical sensor, transport or automatic recovery capability.

## Recent industry and research: new to this reading room

### PRISM: borrow the evaluation protocol first

**Publication context:** University of Michigan's [November 10 CoRL coverage](https://news.engin.umich.edu/2026/11/prism-a-neural-network-component-for-better-robotic-motor-control/) highlights an existing paper, first submitted July 26 and revised October 16. PRISM learns multiplicative interactions in a policy's input representation. [Paper history and method](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.23473).

The authors report **47/50 successful tape pickups versus ACT's 34/50 on an SO-101**, using the same 50 demonstrations. Success required lifting the roll at least 5 cm and holding it for three seconds. They separately report simulated dropped-cube recovery of 23/44 versus 18/44 from shared starting states. These are author-run results; simulated contact-force behavior does not certify physical safety. [Primary results and protocols](https://lsh3163.github.io/prism/).

**RAFTEL relevance:** borrow explicit success criteria, trial denominators, equal training data and shared failure conditions for later evaluation. Our current [evaluation contract](../arm-01/docs/thor-offline-evaluation.md) already keeps failed, missed, assisted and unresolved outcomes visible. Adopting a learning component can wait until the mechanical and sensing gates support a meaningful comparison.

### Gemini Robotics 2.5: kitting is a relevant comparison, not a transferable score

Google DeepMind's **November 10** announcement reports testing across more than 200 tasks and **96% success** for automotive parts kitting with Apollo 2 in a Mercedes-Benz proof-of-concept. Rollout is to robotics partners. These are developer-reported results; the announcement does not supply a trial denominator for that percentage. [Primary announcement](https://deepmind.google/blog/gemini-robotics-2-5-accelerates-real-world-physical-intelligence/).

**RAFTEL relevance:** moving selected objects into a tray is adjacent to BenchSort. Study the task boundaries and outcome verification, but do not compare that percentage directly with our logical sorter or assume access, Thor support or equivalent operating conditions. No stack change is recommended today.

The previously covered [LeRobot v0.7.0 release, November 5](https://huggingface.co/blog/lerobot/lerobot-release-v070), and [NVIDIA announcement, November 9](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/robotics-simulation-advancements-corl/), remain carry-forward items, not new releases since yesterday.

## A ten-minute practical takeaway

Make a short inspection note with four fields: expected model dimensions, observed slicer dimensions, actual machine/material selectors, and warnings or unknowns. If the desktop is unavailable, collect nozzle/spool details and bench measurements instead. This prepares the next existing gate without treating draft files as permission to fabricate.
