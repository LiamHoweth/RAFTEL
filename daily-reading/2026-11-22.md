## What changed in RAFTEL

- [PR #21](https://github.com/LiamHoweth/RAFTEL/pull/21) merged replayable scenes, JSON/SVG outputs, mock placements scored from their recorded destinations, and an offline learning baseline. The [recorded run](../arm-01/docs/learning-research.md) reports **94/100** agreement with the deterministic planner on ten held-out synthetic layouts: **4 of 67** planner-deferred candidates were considered, and **2 of 33** planner-considered candidates were deferred. The learner sees generated features, makes one decision per example and does not control the sorter. The number is not a pick-and-place success rate.
- [PR #22](https://github.com/LiamHoweth/RAFTEL/pull/22) merged a README render of three draft mount-fit coupons. It is a digital geometry illustration, not measured fit. [Draft PR #19](https://github.com/LiamHoweth/RAFTEL/pull/19) is still open and unmerged; its starter-slice, wrist and Art1 results remain separate from main.

## What other teams are measuring

**Author-reported preprint, submitted November 19:** [Mind2Act World v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26444v1) groups 30 simulated manipulation tasks into tests of reasoning from prior observations, execution, and the two working together through feedback. The authors report that the strongest agent reached 52.7% in the reasoning group, 39.0% in the execution group and 12.0% in the combined group. These are results on different task groups in their simulator, not a causal estimate or a hardware comparison. The protocol keeps hidden task state for evaluation, outside the policy input.

**Inference for BenchSort:** keep three questions separate: did the proposed candidate match the screening rule, did the nominal kinematics solver meet its pose tolerance, and did an independent observation establish correct-bin release? A positive answer to the first two does not establish the third.

## Practical takeaway: make failures legible before adding learning

For the planned nominal `base_link` target set, predeclare position and orientation tolerances and record the target, starting joint seed, residuals and solver stop reason. Include a known nominal round trip and retain a limit or singular case without interpreting local failure as proof that the target is globally unreachable. Keep these results separate from logical sorter scores, moving-body clearance and physical placement. No policy or stack change is needed for this step.
