# Code of conduct

RAFTEL welcomes people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. This code applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, and other spaces where people participate on behalf of RAFTEL.

- Treat people with respect. Critique designs and evidence constructively and give contributors credit for their work.
- Explain assumptions, acknowledge uncertainty, and listen when someone raises an accessibility or safety concern.
- Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention, and sharing private information without permission are unacceptable.

Report conduct concerns privately to the [repository maintainer](https://github.com/LiamHoweth) through an established contact channel. If you need a private channel, use GitHub's [abuse reporting controls](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) for relevant content or ask the maintainer for contact details in an issue without identifying the people or incident.

The maintainer may request changes, remove content, restrict participation, or block accounts in response to conduct violations. Share only the information needed to review a report. If a report concerns the maintainer's conduct, use GitHub's reporting controls.
