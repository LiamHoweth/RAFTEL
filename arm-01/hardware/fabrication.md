## Manufacturer reference

The [official product specifications](https://us.anycubic3d.com/products/kobra-3-max), checked November 11, list a **420 × 420 × 500 mm** nominal build volume, **0.4 mm** stock nozzle, **300°C** hotend maximum and **90°C** bed maximum. These are published stock limits, not measured settings or a confirmation of the installed nozzle/profile. Exact revision and accessories are not established.

Use the nominal volume for slicer planning with the correct machine profile and its usable-area limits. Orientation, supports, brims, fits and printed strength need separate checks; capacity alone does not validate a part.
