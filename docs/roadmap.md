# ARM-01 research roadmap

For current evidence, owners and blockers, see the [active handoff](../nextSteps.md). The [requirements](../arm-01/docs/requirements.md) define design targets. Each row below defines the result needed to close a research milestone.

| Milestone | Status | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Reproducible BenchSort | Complete for logical simulation | Seed 42 sorts ten objects into three bins. A supplied scene verifies screening and a bounded grasp failure. Reports reproduce byte-for-byte given identical logical inputs and implementation. The installed wheel runs without dependency acquisition. See the [demo guide](../arm-01/README.md) and [technical audit](research-repository-audit.md). |
| 2. Validated kinematics | In progress | Source frame conventions, FK, finite-difference Jacobian comparisons, bounded local IK and limits have [offline tests](../arm-01/docs/thor-spatial-kinematics.md). Publish a revisioned source-frame target set with prespecified position and orientation tolerances. Record residuals, singular/limit failures and solver seeds. Cross-check nonzero wrist and full-chain markers, and measure the workstation, mount and TCP transforms before claiming coverage of a real bench. |
| 3. Motion planning | In progress | [Timed joint paths](../arm-01/docs/thor-offline-trajectories.md) already check scalar limits under study inputs. Verify intermediate as well as endpoint extrema against declared position, velocity and acceleration bounds. Bind conservative moving-body geometry and obstacles, test known-clear and blocked paths plus between-sample collisions, and keep failed plans and their tolerances. |
| 4. Perception integration | Planned | [Observation replay](../arm-01/docs/offline-observation-replay.md) exercises frame, time and uncertainty checks. Collect timestamped images and measured targets across the bench. Version intrinsics and directed extrinsics. On held-out calibration points and scenes, report localization error, detection misses, rejection coverage and stale or occluded observations against preregistered task tolerances. |
| 5. Learning-based manipulation | Planned | The [synthetic waypoint screening experiment](../arm-01/docs/learning-research.md) establishes a training/evaluation procedure. A manipulation milestone requires a versioned environment with contact and dynamics or suitable recorded demonstrations, explicit actions and episode outcomes, held-out objects and layouts, and comparison against deterministic and trivial baselines across independent seeds. Report false accepts, failed grasps, timeouts and safety interventions alongside task success. |
| 6. Physical ARM-01 | Blocked on design and commissioning | Close the [mechanical/electrical gates](#physical-dependencies). Execute supervised fixed-location pick/place before variable-position sorting. The [benchmark protocol](../arm-01/docs/verification-plan.md#proposed-90-target-protocol) calls for ten independently reset ten-object layouts and targets at least 90% observed successful cycles, with independent placement verification, attempts/coverage, assistance, faults and uncertainty reported. |

## Physical dependencies

1. **Design and fabrication review.** Use the revisioned Thor CAD baseline, preserve six independent arm axes plus a motorized parallel gripper, and document the seven arm motor channels and coupled shoulder. Close original Art1 editability, wrist contact and jaw closure, actuator sizing and thermal duty, mount loads and print fit. Record measured as-built geometry, mass and clearances on approved artifacts. [CAD reuse decision](architecture-decisions/0001-mechanical-starting-point.md) · [Fabrication gates](../arm-01/hardware/fabrication.md)
2. **Electrical and controller review.** Confirm exact motor and driver variants, wiring clearances, coupled-channel calibration, actual-state feedback, fault latching and loss-of-feedback response. Prove independent motor-energy interruption and controlled gravity retention with restraint before restricted powered tests. [Commissioning checklist](../arm-01/docs/safety.md)
3. **Manipulation and reliability.** Verify individual-joint motion before full-arm motion. Collect fixed-location grasp, transport and release measurements, then run supervised variable-position trials. Design the detachable mount for repeatable reconnect and recalibration. Assess mobile-clearance ideas separately after the bench platform is stable. [Verification plan](../arm-01/docs/verification-plan.md) · [Mobile integration interface](../arm-01/docs/mobile-integration.md)

Purchases, fabrication and physical operation require explicit owner approval. Every accepted gate needs a versioned configuration, runnable procedure, retained failures and evidence appropriate to the claim.
